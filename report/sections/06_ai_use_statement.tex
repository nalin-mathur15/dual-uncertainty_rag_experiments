\section{AI Use Statement}
\label{sec:ai_use_statement}

In accordance with the assessment guidelines of SC4001 Neural Networks and Deep Learning, we declare the use of generative AI tools in the preparation of this group project:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Tools and Models Employed:} We utilized Google Antigravity / Gemini 3.8 and Claude for architectural brainstorming, SLURM bash scripting assistance, LaTeX formatting, and code debugging (specifically resolving PyTorch 2.5 CUDA kernel compatibility on the NTU CCDS TC1 Tesla V100 GPU cluster).
    \item \textbf{Verification and Validation:} All mathematical derivations, loss formulation bounds, and experimental metrics were independently verified and audited by team members against the underlying benchmark logs. All literature citations were verified against original published venue sources (ICLR, EMNLP, ACL, NeurIPS).
    \item \textbf{Team Contributions and Decisions:} Our team independently selected the research question (Reliable RAG Direction F), conceptualized the $2 \times 2$ epistemic decoupling framework, designed the counterfactual Value of Information (VOI) formulation, implemented the neural architectures, executed all SLURM cluster training jobs, and interpreted the experimental results.
\end{itemize}
